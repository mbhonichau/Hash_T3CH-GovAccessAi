Programme notice configuration

Set the official instructions applicants will see when they receive a decision.

What should these instructions say?

This wording is added to applicant decision notices. Only enter details confirmed in the programme's official rules.

  • How long the applicant has to ask for a review or appeal, and when that period starts.
  • How to submit the request, including the approved email, address, office or online service.
  • What information or supporting documents the applicant should include.
  • Where the applicant can get help or ask questions.

Do not guess a deadline, contact detail or right of appeal. Check the current official programme rules before saving.
